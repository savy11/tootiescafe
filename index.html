Our Heritage

Where Everyone Feels Like Family Right Away

Tootie and Harry Brusnahan started with a simple dream: serving honest, home-cooked meals to their neighbors. After opening their first diner in 1972 and bringing Tootie's to historic Main Street in New Egypt in 1980, that same family pride continues every single morning.

Whether you're grabbing a stool at the counter for a quick breakfast, gathering the family in our big corner booth, or stopping by after a backyard BBQ for fresh-out-of-the-oven baked goods and two-scoop sundaes, you're always among friends at Tootie's.
